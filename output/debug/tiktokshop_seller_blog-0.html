Introduction to Seller Fees

08/04/2026

Applies to:Vietnam
TikTok Shop will adjust the fixed commission fees for certain product categories & transaction fee.

Overview

Type of Seller Fee
Description
Platform Commission Fee
An amount charged for each successful order, based on rates that differ for each product sub-category.
The Platform Commission Fee rate is different for Mall sellers and Marketplace sellers.
Transaction FeeAn amount charged for processing successful orders
Shipping FeeAn amount paid to the logistics provider for successful order delivery. Shipping fee is only incurred to sellers if the actual package measurements exceed the information sellers has provided in product listing.
Order Processing Fee
An amount that the platform charges for order-based service maintenance and enhancement on the platform

Platform Commission Fee

After an order is successfully delivered to buyers, the platform will automatically deduct a fee from the final order/payout settlement. This is the Platform Commission Fee, and is charged based on rates that differ for each product sub-category.
The Platform Commission Fee rate is different for Marketplace Sellers and Mall Sellers.

Learn more about the Mall program here.

Transaction Fee

After an order is successfully delivered to buyers, TikTok Shop will automatically deduct a fee from the final order/payout settlement. This is the Transaction Fee, and is charged to all sellers for processing successful orders in TikTok Shop.
  • From 09th May, 2026: The Transaction Fee rate is 6.00%.*
For more details on the Transaction Fee and how the fee is calculated, please visit this article.

Order Processing Fee

The Order Processing Fee is an amount that the platform charges all sellers in Vietnam for the processing of the order on the platform. The Order Processing Fee is in place to strategically enhance long-term development and services. This is evident in continuous logistics optimization, AI assistant feature, and more. These improvements lay the foundation for sustainable growth and strengthen the value delivered to TikTok Shop consumers and sellers.
The fee is charged starting from 00:00, 27th October 2025, Vietnam time (GMT+7).
The Order Processing Fee is fixed at VND 3000 (tax inclusive) per successfully delivered order, regardless of how many items are placed under the order.
For more details on the Order Processing Fee, please visit this article.

Shipping Fee

Shipping fee* is the fee paid to the platform logistic service providers for order delivery.
TikTok Shop's works with logistics service providers to deliver the order to the customer and fulfill the order are considered a fulfillment service provided to the sellers. Hence, seller shipping fee is treated as a fee upon the completion of the fulfillment service.
Shipping fee is only incurred to sellers if the actual package measurements exceed the information sellers has provided in product listing.*Not to be confused with SFP service feeFor more details on the Shipping Fee, please visit this article.
*Important Note: The Marketplace Commission Fee, Transaction Fee and Shipping Fee are inclusive of VN taxes. If you are a tax registered organisation in Vietnam and your Tax Code has been provided to and verified by TikTok, we will only collect the amount exclusive of taxes. Learn more here.
